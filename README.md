# Universe Creator — Simulation Rules

## 1. Stars
- Players can place unlimited stars.
- Three star types are available: G, K and M.
- Stars attract nearby celestial bodies through gravity.
- Stars can absorb planets and increase in mass and size.
- When two stars collide, they combine to form a black hole.

## 2. Planets
- Players can place unlimited planets.
- Four planet types are available: Rocky, Gas, Ice and Dwarf.
- Planets automatically orbit the nearest star when placed.
- If no star exists, planets start without an initial velocity.
- Planets cannot be placed overlapping other celestial bodies.

## 3. Gravity and Orbits
- All celestial bodies exert gravitational attraction on one another.
- Gravitational strength depends on mass and distance.
- Multiple celestial bodies can influence each other's movement.
- Planetary orbits can change or become unstable due to gravitational interactions.

## 4. Star Attraction Zone
- Every star has an attraction zone extending two star radii beyond its surface.
- Planets experience stronger attraction when entering this zone.
- The attraction increases as planets move closer to the star.

## 5. Planet Collisions
- When two planets of different masses collide, the smaller planet is removed.
- The larger planet survives and changes its movement.
- When two planets of equal mass collide, both survive and deflect.

## 6. Star Collisions
- When a planet collides with a star, the star absorbs it and grows.
- When two stars collide, they form a black hole.
- The newly formed black hole inherits their combined mass and momentum.

## 7. Black Holes
- Players can place black holes anywhere without overlapping existing bodies.
- Black holes can also form from collisions between stars.
- Black holes attract other celestial bodies through gravity.
- Black holes absorb planets and stars.
- Absorbing other bodies increases their mass and size.
- When two black holes collide, the more massive one absorbs the smaller one.

## 8. Simulation Controls
- **Play:** Starts or resumes the simulation.
- **Pause:** Pauses the simulation.
- **Reset:** Clears the simulation.
- **Left Click:** Places the selected celestial body.

## 9. General Rules
- There is no fixed limit on the number of celestial bodies.
- Celestial bodies can move outside the visible simulation area.
- The simulation uses scaled distances, masses and time.
- All celestial bodies follow the simulation's gravitational and collision rules.
